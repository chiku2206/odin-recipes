Pizza Recipe

This is a delicious pizza recipe that you can try at home. Follow the steps below to make a perfect pizza.

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish loved by people all around the world. It is made with soft dough, tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and different toppings such as onions and capsicum. Pizza can be prepared with many varieties of vegetables, cheese, and sauces. It is baked until the crust becomes golden and crispy and the cheese melts perfectly. Pizza is delicious and is often enjoyed with oregano and chilli flakes.

Ingredients
  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Onion
  • Capsicum
  • Oregano
  • Chilli flakes

steps:
  1. Step 1: Prepare the pizza dough and roll it into a round shape.
  2. Step 2: Spread tomato sauce evenly over the pizza dough.
  3. Step 3: Add grated mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  4. Step 4: Place sliced onions and capsicum on top.
  5. Step 5: Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes according to taste.
  6. Step 6: Bake the pizza until the crust is golden and the cheese melts.
  7. Step 7: Remove the pizza, cut it into slices, and serve hot.
